# Anthropic IPO: got shares? Selling all on day one is safest; to aim for more, sell within 10 days. Didn't? Don't chase it on day one.

We studied 60 of the biggest IPOs in history. All 30 that were priced the way Anthropic is expected to be priced closed their first day at or above the IPO price, but people who bought after trading started usually did not make money. Forecast: ANT closes day one 15% to 25% above its IPO price.

Published before Anthropic prices its IPO (expected 2026-11-18) and starts trading on Nasdaq as ANT (expected 2026-11-19), so it can be checked afterwards. Full report: [`report.md`](report.md). Chinese version: [`報告.md`](報告.md).

| # | Prediction | Similar past IPOs inside this range |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First-day return **+15% to +25%**, central +20% | 4 of 30 |
| 2 | The highest price of the first 60 trading days comes **on or before trading day 10** (base rate 55%) | 16 of 29 |

**Scoring:** R = Nasdaq Official Closing Price on the first day of regular trading ÷ final IPO price in the 424B4 − 1, unrounded; band edges count as hits; no partial credit. Void if ANT has not started trading on Nasdaq by 2026-12-31. Full rules are in `report.md`.
